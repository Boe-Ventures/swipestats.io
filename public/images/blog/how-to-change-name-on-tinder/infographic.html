How to change your name on Tinder
Three routes out of the name you typed at 1 AM
Your name locks at signup and there is no edit button. These are the only three methods that work, ranked by what they cost you.
Method 01 · easiest
Change it on Facebook
Only works if the account was created through Facebook in the first place.
  • 1Facebook, Settings, Personal Information, Name, Edit.
  • 2Tinder, Settings, scroll down, Logout.
  • 3Log back in with Facebook.
Updates within 24 hours
Method 02 · underrated
Email Tinder Support
Free, no risk, and almost nobody bothers trying it first.
  • 1Open the Tinder help center.
  • 2Submit a request, general inquiry.
  • 3Say exactly what is wrong, in one line.
Best odds: typos, legal name changes, or a name pulled wrong from your Apple ID.
Takes about 5 minutes
Method 03 · nuclear
Delete and start over
The only guaranteed route if you signed up with a phone number, email, or Google.
  • 1Profile icon, then Settings.
  • 2Scroll to the bottom, Delete Account.
  • 3Decline the offer to "pause" instead.
Ask support about moving your subscription before you confirm, not after.
Costs all matches, messages, likes
