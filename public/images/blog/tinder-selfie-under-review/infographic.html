Selfie under review
How long does a Tinder selfie review take?
Four windows, from routine to purgatory. You are locked out for all of them.
5 to 30 minutes
Normal automated review
The AI compared your face to your photos and moved on. Boring, in the best possible way.
24 to 48 hours
Extra processing needed
Weird lighting, a new beard, or backed-up servers on a peak evening. Still routine.
Several days to weeks
Manual review queue
The AI could not confidently match you, so a human now has your case.
Indefinite
Stuck in purgatory
No rejection, no explanation, no movement. Contact support after 48 to 72 hours.
