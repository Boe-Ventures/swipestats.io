The Hinge shadowban checklist
Six signs Hinge has quietly put you on mute
Hinge confirmed it hides "bad actors" from search, feed and messages. Here is what that looks like from your side of the screen.
Sign 01
Your likes fell off a cliff
Sudden, not gradual. A steady trickle one week, tumbleweeds the next.
Sign 02
Every match goes silent at once
Not one ghost. All of them. They may not be able to see your messages.
Sign 03
The same faces on repeat
The algorithm has stopped feeding you fresh profiles.
Sign 04
Zero engagement on comments
Thoughtful prompt comments, specific photo likes, nothing back. Not even a pity like.
Sign 05 · the Boost test
11x reach, still nothing
A Boost shows you to 11x more people. Zero likes after one means you are being suppressed.
Sign 06 · Most Compatible
8x picks turned random
Most Compatible users are 8x more likely to get a date. Random picks mean a downgrade.
The benchmark
Under 2 likes a week and under 1 match a week means something is wrong. A first offence is usually a 3 day visibility cut, so serve the timeout before you nuke the account.
