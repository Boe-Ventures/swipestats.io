## Why Your Hinge Feed Looks Like a Post-Apocalyptic Wasteland

Let's talk about why you're here instead of, you know, on a date.

Hinge uses your preferences and activity to help decide who you see. Think of it as a matchmaker that judges you silently every time you open the app. The [algorithm guide](/blog/hinge-algorithm) explains the information Hinge discloses, including Likes, skips, matches, and Dealbreakers. It also explains why the internet's confident talk about a single desirability score goes beyond what we can verify.

Your feed can become repetitive when your filters leave a small pool. Look at distance first, then age and Dealbreakers. If you are asking for someone within a mile, of one exact age, who shares six specific lifestyle choices, the available crowd may be smaller than the queue at your local coffee shop.

There is also the profile itself. People decide whether they want to Like what you show them. If your prompts say very little and your photos were taken on a potato, a new account gives you another place to display the same problem. Painful to hear. Considerably cheaper to fix than buying another phone.

You may have seen many of the people who currently fit your settings. The feed starts recycling faces like a sad Spotify playlist. That experience does not establish your ranking or tell you that everyone nearby has rejected you. Broader preferences can change who is eligible to appear; better photos help when those people see you.

This is what sends people googling "how to reset Hinge" at midnight. Work out whether you want different recommendations, a better profile, or some time away from dating apps. Each gives you a different reason to touch the settings.

## How to Reset Hinge with Fresh Start

Some accounts receive a Fresh Start offer in the settings or deletion flow. Users have described it as a way to refresh recommendations while keeping the account. Availability can change. Treat the screen in your app as the source of truth for what you are accepting.

### Step-by-Step: Check the Offer Before Confirming

1. Open Hinge and go to your profile, then **Settings**.
2. Look for Fresh Start or an offer in the account-management flow.
3. Read the confirmation, especially what happens to Likes, matches, and conversations.
4. Accept only if that is the action you want. If the screen says it will delete your account, stop there while you decide.

People post menu paths as if every phone, region, and app version sees the same screen. They don't. A blog telling you to select a particular deletion reason cannot guarantee the offer will appear. Clicking through quickly is a spectacularly bad experiment when the button says Delete Account.

### What Gets Reset?

Check the offer's own list. Save a screenshot if you want to remember what it promised. A refresh may bring previously seen profiles back into view, but that does not tell you which internal recommendation signals changed.

Look specifically for whether pending Likes are affected. If there is a Like you want to respond to or a conversation you care about, deal with it before experimenting. Nobody wants their explanation for a missed connection to be "I was trying to optimize my settings."

Fresh Start has been discussed as a way to revisit recommendations without throwing away the account. We cannot promise every implementation keeps or removes exactly the same items. Your confirmation screen has the final say.

### The Boost Window and Cooldown Claims

You'll hear stories about ten matches in ten minutes and a magical 24-72-hour window. A good result after a refresh is a good result. It doesn't prove everyone gets the same boost or reveal how Hinge allocated visibility.

The same applies to the often-repeated 30-day cooldown. Hinge does not publish a universal schedule in the official sources reviewed here. If the feature is unavailable, update the app and check again later or ask support. Building a monthly ritual around an undocumented timer is an ambitious amount of admin for a dating app.

### If Fresh Start Doesn't Appear

Don't keep pressing deletion buttons to coax it out. You can still edit your photos and prompts, review filters, or pause. Those choices are useful even when your account never receives a Fresh Start offer.

If you think something is malfunctioning, record the app version and what you see. Send support a description of the missing control or error. "My results are awful" describes the mood; an error message describes something they can investigate.

## How to Refresh Your Hinge Feed Without Deleting Anything

Maybe you don't want to use Fresh Start. Maybe the option isn't there. Maybe you just enjoy making things harder for yourself, a personality trait that probably extends to your dating life. Let's try the controls that are already in front of you.

### The 1km Radius Hack: What It Actually Changes

You will find people recommending that you shrink the distance to one kilometer, Like selectively, and gradually expand it. Shrinking the radius changes the local pool. The claim that this wipes an algorithm score has no published support.

Use distance to answer a real question: how far would you travel for a date? Try that range and see whether the recommendations make sense. A kilometer in central London and a kilometer in rural Norway give you very different dating prospects. The second one might mostly contain sheep, and they aren't filling out Hinge prompts.

If someone outside that radius would still be worth meeting, reconsider whether distance should be a hard Dealbreaker. Keep the preferences that matter to your actual life. Turning every preference into a requirement can leave the app with very little to show you.

### Make Your Choices More Useful

- **Review your preferences.** Update age, distance, and relationship intentions when they no longer match what you want. Change one setting at a time if you want to understand the difference.
- **Send Likes to people you want to meet.** A limited daily allowance is no reason to Like profiles you would ignore if they replied.
- **Write a relevant comment.** Mention a detail from the profile. Give the other person something easier to answer than an unexplained heart.
- **Refresh your photos and prompts.** An edit changes what people see. Hinge does not promise a visibility boost every time you swap a sentence.

### Pause When You Need a Break

If you are tired of the whole exercise, use Pause in Settings. This lets you take time away while keeping the account and existing conversations. Check the current screen for how outstanding Likes are handled.

Pausing can be a useful way to stop endlessly fiddling with the profile. Go do something that could eventually become a better photo or a better story. You can give the app a rest without turning your account into a science project.

## The Nuclear Option: Delete the Account

Deletion is available when you want to remove the account. It costs you the profile and its conversations. There is no guaranteed new-user boost waiting on the other side, so make the choice around what you want to keep and what you want to leave behind.

### Before You Delete

Request your data through **Settings > Download My Data** if you want a record of your own activity. Wait for it to arrive and save it before deleting. The export contains your side of conversations, so it isn't a complete backup of every chat.

If you have a date in progress, agree on another way to stay in touch. Save the prompt answers and photos you might use again. Check where your subscription was purchased and cancel through the relevant provider if you want billing to stop.

App Store and Google Play subscriptions need separate cancellation. Hinge says deleting an account cancels subscriptions purchased through its direct payment system. Verify the confirmation for your own purchase. An account deletion should not turn into a recurring donation to a dating app.

### Step-by-Step: Delete Hinge

1. Sign in to Hinge.
2. Open the Photo icon, then the Gear icon for **Account Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Delete Account**.
4. Follow the confirmation prompts and check that deletion completes.

Uninstalling the app leaves the account in place. Hinge removes account information according to its privacy policy after proper deletion, with retention rules for things such as safety obligations. Deletion should not be understood as an instant erasure of every record the company has ever held.

### Returning Later

If you are eligible to use Hinge and choose to return, prepare the profile first. Use current photos and prompts you can stand behind. Work out what kind of person you want to meet so you can set your preferences honestly.

A new device or phone number cannot guarantee better recommendations. The internet's photo-hash tricks and verification waiting periods are guesses about systems we cannot inspect. Complete verification when the app requires it. If verification fails, use support rather than building an elaborate workaround around a theory.

### If You Were Banned

Use the Appeal option on the ban screen. Explain what happened and follow the verification steps the process requests. Recreating accounts to get around the restriction does not resolve the underlying decision. Our [Hinge ban guide](/blog/hinge-ban) covers the appeal route.

## Fresh Start, Profile Refresh, or Deletion?

| Option | What you're trying to change | What to check first |
|---|---|---|
| Profile refresh | Photos, prompts, preferences | Save a baseline if you want to compare results |
| Fresh Start offer | Recommendations within an existing account | Read the exact confirmation and consequences |
| Pause | Time away from new recommendations | Billing and any conversations you want to continue |
| Delete | Remove the account | Data download, conversations, subscription provider |

Most people arriving here with a tired feed should start with a profile refresh and preference review. Give the changes time. Deleting every conversation is a high price for an experiment you could begin by replacing a blurry photo.

## What to Fix Before You Reset (Or You'll Be Back Here in 3 Weeks)

If you return with the same photos, the same prompts, and the same habits, people have much the same information to judge. A reset opens another opportunity to present yourself. Your profile is what climbs through it. Or faceplants on the windowsill. Your call.

### Fix Your Photos First

Your photos make a substantial first impression. Make each one earn its place. Here's the minimum:

- **No group shots as your first photo.** She's not going to play detective to figure out which one you are. She'll just skip you entirely.
- **No sunglasses in every pic.** One outdoor shot with shades? Fine. Every photo looking like you're in witness protection? No.
- **At least one clear face shot.** Eyes visible, decent lighting, not a zoomed-in crop from a wedding photo where you can still see your cousin's elbow.
- **Show your life, not your bathroom mirror.** Activity shots. A photo of you cooking. You with friends. You doing literally anything besides holding your phone at arm's length.

Need actual examples? Check out [the best Hinge profiles](/blog/best-hinge-profiles-for-guys) and steal ideas from people who are doing it right.

### Fix Your Prompts

Generic prompts get generic results. "I'm looking for someone who doesn't take themselves too seriously" is the prompt equivalent of a plain white t-shirt. It fits everyone and excites nobody.

Be specific. Be funny. Be revealing. "I'll know it's real when you let me control the aux cord without judging my Taylor Swift phase" tells someone a hundred times more about you than "I value honesty and good vibes." If you need inspiration, read the guide on [best Hinge prompts](/blog/best-hinge-prompts) and stop writing your prompts like a corporate mission statement.

### Fix Your Swiping Habits

Choose people you would want to meet and send [thoughtful openers](/blog/how-to-start-a-conversation-on-hinge) with your Likes. Reference something specific: their terrible karaoke song, the place in the hiking photo, the prompt answer you want to argue with. This gives them a conversation to join. A published hidden-score bonus is unnecessary to explain why that is useful.

### The Pre-Reset Checklist

Before you refresh or recreate the profile, make sure you've got:

1. **6 strong photos.** Varied backgrounds, clear face, at least one full body, at least one activity shot.
2. **3 killer prompts.** Specific, funny, or thought-provoking. Ideally all three.
3. **A swiping plan.** You will be selective this time. You will send comments with your likes. You will not revert to swiping like a casino slot machine the second you see a pretty face.

Give your changes a fair comparison. Note when you made them, keep your activity roughly similar, and look at a few weeks of results. Changing everything after every quiet evening makes it impossible to tell which version people responded to.

## FAQ

### How often can you use Fresh Start on Hinge?

Availability varies. The popular 30-day rule is not a universal schedule published in the official help pages reviewed here. Follow what your account offers. If you want to reset every month, spend some of that energy improving the profile you keep recreating.

### Does Hinge Fresh Start reset your likes?

Read the confirmation on your account before accepting. Do not assume pending Likes survive a feed refresh. Respond to any you care about first and save a screenshot of the terms if you want a record.

### Can you start again with the same phone number?

An eligible user can follow Hinge's current sign-up flow. Reusing or changing the number provides no guaranteed ranking outcome. If you have a ban, use the appeal process. A SIM card does not settle an account dispute.

### What happens when you delete your account?

You lose the account's profile content and conversations, with data handled under Hinge's privacy policy. Check your subscription provider separately. Store billing can continue until you cancel it there.

### Does resetting clear your Elo score?

Hinge does not publish a personal Elo score or a guaranteed deletion-based score reset. Fresh Start and profile edits can change the experience without telling you what happened inside the recommendation system.

### Does deleting the app delete your profile?

No. To remove the account, use Settings and complete Delete Account. Uninstalling is like leaving a room and assuming the furniture disappeared. Your profile still needs its own decision.

### What should I change first?

Start with a clear first photo and a prompt someone can answer. Ask a brutally honest friend to review the result, the kind who will tell you that your third prompt makes you sound like a serial killer. Everyone needs one of those friends.
