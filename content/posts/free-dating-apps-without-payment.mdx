- **Facebook Dating, OkCupid, Hinge, and Plenty of Fish** let you send a free introduction or comment before a match, within each app's limits.
- **All seven apps below** let you chat with matches for free. Bumble now lets either person start the conversation.

## What you can do for free on each dating app

A free chat with a match and a free first message before matching are two different things. Here's how the seven apps compare, based on their help pages and product announcements reviewed in November 2026. Features can vary by country and account.

| App | Chat after a match | Start before a match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook Dating | Free | You can comment when you like someone |
| Tinder | Free | Tinder's standard free chat starts after a mutual match |
| Hinge | Free | Add a comment to a Like or Rose |
| Bumble | Free | Either person can start the standard chat after matching |
| OkCupid | Free | Send an Intro with your Like |
| Plenty of Fish | Free | Send a limited number of First Contacts to eligible profiles |
| Coffee Meets Bagel | Free, with an expiration window | Standard chat opens after a mutual match |

## Facebook Dating for a free experience inside Facebook

[Meta describes Facebook Dating as free](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/facebook-dating-adds-features-address-swipe-fatigue/). You can [comment on a profile or send a Like](https://about.fb.com/news/2019/09/facebook-dating/) and chat when you match. You'll need a Facebook account and access to Dating in the Facebook mobile app. If you'd rather leave Facebook out of your dating life, try OkCupid or Hinge.

## Tinder for free chat after matching

[Tinder's free version](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647686-Tinder-Overview) lets you like profiles, match, and chat. Standard chat opens when you both like each other. A subscription can add perks such as unlimited Likes, but the free version covers the basics. See our [Tinder review](/blog/tinder-review) for more on the paid tiers.

## Hinge for sending a comment with a Like

Hinge lets you [add a comment to a Like](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/36311632894995-Likes) to start with something more specific than a heart. Free members have a daily Like limit and can see incoming Likes one at a time. You also get [one free Rose each week](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/36311177115027-Roses), which you can use in Standouts. If you match, you can chat for free.

## Bumble for conversations either person can start

On Bumble, [either person can send the first message](https://support.bumble.com/hc/en-us/articles/36679912947229-How-conversations-start-on-Bumble). Bumble gives you 72 hours from matching to start the conversation. Once one person sends an opener, the other has 72 hours to reply. Both opening messages are free. If you're used to Bumble's old women-message-first rule, this is the part that changed.

## OkCupid for sending a free Intro

[OkCupid's free account](https://okcupid-app.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/22945125907227-OkCupid-Paid-Features-Subscriptions-Basic-Premium-Incognito-and-Premium-Plus) lets you like profiles, send Intros, and chat with matches. An Intro is a first message attached to a Like, so you can mention something specific on their profile before they like you back. Free accounts can see incoming Intros one at a time.

## Plenty of Fish for limited first messages before matching

[Plenty of Fish](https://help.pof.com/hc/en-us/articles/41814438537371-First-Contacts-and-sending-messages) lets you message a mutual match directly. You can also send a First Contact before matching, subject to recipient preferences and your daily allowance. Check the allowance in your account before choosing Plenty of Fish for this feature.

## Coffee Meets Bagel for a smaller set of profiles

[Coffee Meets Bagel](https://help.ww.coffeemeetsbagel.com/hc/en-us/articles/33782916143635-About-Coffee-Meets-Bagel) offers a daily batch of suggested profiles. You can chat after a mutual match. [Chats initially stay open for seven days](https://coffeemeetsbagel.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020787834-How-do-I-extend-a-chat), and a message close to expiry extends the chat. If a chat closes, reopening it costs beans, the app's currency.

## When is a paid tier worth trying?

Start with the part you're trying to improve. If your problem is running out of Likes, a plan with unlimited Likes might help you reach more people. If you're already getting matches and the conversations go nowhere, try changing your opener first. Give yourself a week on the free tier and count matches and conversations before deciding whether an upgrade is worth it.

## FAQ

### Which dating apps let you message without paying?

Facebook Dating, Tinder, Hinge, Bumble, OkCupid, Plenty of Fish, and Coffee Meets Bagel let you chat with matches on a free account. Bumble and Coffee Meets Bagel have chat deadlines to watch for.

### Can you send a message without matching first?

Facebook Dating lets you comment on profiles, OkCupid lets you send an Intro, Hinge lets you attach a comment to a Like or Rose, and Plenty of Fish lets you send a limited number of First Contacts. Each feature gives you a way to introduce yourself before matching.

### Do free dating apps require a credit card?

You can use the free features above without purchasing a subscription. If an app offers a free trial of a paid plan, check the renewal terms before accepting it.
