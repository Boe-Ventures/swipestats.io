Registered accounts, monthly active users, and payers measure different things. Someone who signed up years ago can still count as registered, while an active user has used the app during the measurement period. Match Group counts a payer in a month when the brand earns direct revenue from that person. That includes subscriptions and one-off purchases.

## How to measure your Hinge match rate

To measure how often the Likes you send turn into matches, divide the number of those Likes that became matches by the number of Likes you sent. If 5 of 100 Likes became matches, your outbound match rate for those Likes is 5%.

Use the date you sent each Like to define the sample and give recent Likes time to turn into matches. Keep incoming Likes you accepted separate. Someone else's decision to send you a Like answers a different question from whether someone matched after your Like.

SwipeStats benchmarks use exports that users choose to upload. People who upload their data can differ from the wider Hinge population. A useful comparison specifies the cohort, sample size, and date range. The company results above describe Hinge's business and come from Match Group's reporting.

## What Hinge publishes about getting to a date

[Hinge reports](https://hinge.co/newsroom/convo-starters) that Likes sent with a comment were twice as likely to lead to a date as Likes sent without one. This is a relative comparison in Hinge's data. Hinge's announcement gives no baseline percentage for how many Likes turned into dates.

## FAQ

<FAQ>

### How many people use Hinge?

Match Group reported double-digit year-over-year user growth for Hinge in Q3 2026. The release gives the direction of growth without a global count of registered accounts.

### What is the average match rate on Hinge?

Match Group's earnings releases don't provide a platform-wide match rate. For your outbound rate, use the share of Likes you sent that became matches. Compare rates only when the denominator and time period are similar.

### How many Likes can you send on Hinge for free?

[Hinge says](https://help.hinge.co/hc/en-us/articles/38014282744595-Subscription-and-Purchase-Benefits) free users can send a limited number of Likes each day and see incoming Likes one at a time. Hinge+ and HingeX include unlimited Likes. Everyone gets one free Rose each week.
