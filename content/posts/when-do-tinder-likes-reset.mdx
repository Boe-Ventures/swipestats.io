## When Do Tinder Likes Reset?

If you didn't already get, Tinder like resets:

- **Free users:** Your likes reset every 24 hours, so you can start swiping right on all the wrong people all over again.
- **Premium users:** You get unlimited likes, so you can swipe right on every profile you see without restraint. Congrats, you're officially a desperate mess.
  The reset time can vary, but it's usually 24 hours from your last like or at midnight if you still think the world revolves around you. Tinder never publishes an exact number in its [FAQ](https://tinder.com/faq), so the daily cap you get is whatever the app decides you deserve.

## The "Likes You" Feature

Tinder Gold and [Platinum](/blog/tinder-platinum) users can see the profiles of people who've already liked them, because apparently, false hope is a premium feature now.

Enjoy staring at those likes until they expire or you work up the courage to make the first move (spoiler alert: you won't).

This feature doesn't reset. You can see these likes until you act on them or they expire.

## The Truth About "Resetting" Your Account

You might have heard about "resetting" your Tinder account to get a fresh start. While this used to be a popular tactic, Tinder has cracked down on this practice.

Attempting to reset your account by deleting and recreating it can lead to shadowbanning, where your profile becomes invisible to others without your knowledge.

You now have to go through steps like getting a new phone or waiting months for Tinder to delete your data.
