<TLDR>

### TL;DR: Check the countdown

Run out of free likes? Open Tinder and check the countdown it shows you. A universal midnight or 24-hour promise would be guessing.

- Plus, Gold, and Platinum include unlimited likes.
- Deleting your account removes your matches. It is a poor way to troubleshoot a like limit.
- Put the wait to use: [improve your profile](/swipeguide) before your next swiping session.

</TLDR>

## The Basics of Tinder Likes

You use a like when you swipe right on someone. Free accounts have an allowance, so an enthusiastic swiping session can leave you staring at a paywall. Tinder would love you to solve that problem with your wallet.

![Tinder homepage with the headline It starts with a swipe and a Get the app button](/images/blog/when-do-tinder-likes-reset/shot-1.png)

## When Do Tinder Likes Reset?

Your app's reset countdown is the useful answer. Check the message shown when you run out, then come back when that timer ends. Account offers and limits can change, so a number copied from an old article may not match what you see.

If the countdown has ended and you still cannot like anyone, update the app and check [Tinder support](https://www.help.tinder.com/). A like-limit message by itself does not establish a shadowban.

![Tinder FAQ page with a Profile and Matches section](/images/blog/when-do-tinder-likes-reset/shot-2.png)

## Make Your Likes Count

Read the profile before you swipe. Use a like on someone you would want to meet; throwing one at every face burns through your allowance fast.

Your photos deserve some attention too. A clear first photo and a bio that gives someone an easy question to ask are useful improvements while you wait. See our [profile guide](/swipeguide) for examples.

A [paid subscription](/blog/is-tinder-gold-worth-it) can remove the like allowance. Whether that convenience is worth the price depends on how often you use it. More likes still leave you with the same profile.

## Does the “Likes You” List Reset Too?

Gold and [Platinum](/blog/tinder-platinum) let you see people who have already liked you. That list is a separate feature from your outgoing like allowance. The countdown you see after running out of likes is about your ability to send more.

## Should You Reset Your Account?

Deleting your account removes your matches and messages. Tinder explains the consequences in its [account deletion guide](https://www.help.tinder.com/hc/en-us/articles/6956972185229-Delete-your-Tinder-account). Waiting for the timer is a much smaller commitment.

![Tinder support page with troubleshooting and safety sections](/images/blog/when-do-tinder-likes-reset/shot-3.png)

If a limit appears broken, send support the exact message and the time you saw it. That gives them something concrete to investigate.
